What is this page?

This is your Upcoming Projects page. Here, you can plan future art projects by listing their name, purpose, and details. You can also upload a picture to visualize your goal — click on any image to enlarge it. All entries are saved automatically in your browser.

Any project you add here will also appear as an option on the Wishlist page. This allows you to associate specific supplies with your upcoming projects when adding items to your wishlist.
