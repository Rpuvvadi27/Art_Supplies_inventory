What is this page?

This is your Wishlist page where you can track art supplies you plan to purchase. Use the table to view each item by name, category, associated project, and an optional product link. You can edit or delete any item using the buttons in the "Actions" column.

When adding an item, you can select a related project from the dropdown if it corresponds to something on your Upcoming Projects list. If you have a specific product in mind, you can also include a clickable link to that item in the "Product Link" field.

At the bottom of the page, you'll find a chatbot. You can ask it basic art-related questions about materials like paint, brushes, beads, yarn, thread, and more. It can offer tips, product suggestions, or help you understand what tools are best for your creative projects.
