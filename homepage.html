What is this page?

This is your Inventory page where you can track the supplies you own and how you feel about them. Use the table to view each product by name, brand, and category. The category field includes a dropdown menu to help you describe or classify the product easily.

You can also add where the product was purchased from—handy for finding it again if you liked it. There is also a review field for jotting down your thoughts, so you can quickly see whether it's something you'd want to buy again.

Every entry in the table can be edited or deleted at any time using the buttons in the "Actions" column, so your list stays current and useful.
